## Featured Research & Projects

### D-HAQNAS — Differentiable Hardware-Aware Quantum Neural Architecture Search

**Qiskit • PennyLane • PyTorch • NISQ Optimization • Quantum ML**

Designed a differentiable super-circuit framework for joint optimization of quantum gate topology and variational parameters under real NISQ hardware constraints.

- Achieved **31.5% sparsification** while maintaining **96.21% accuracy** on PneumoniaMNIST
- Demonstrated **+4.17% statistically significant gain** on RetinaMNIST (p = 0.031) via hybrid classical–quantum architecture with weighted residual fusion
- Simulated realistic IBM Quantum noise models, achieving **98.9% performance retention**

> *Bridging quantum hardware constraints with differentiable learning.*
